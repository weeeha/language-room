Coffee at a Lisbon café

Place
Pastelaria in Alfama. A small pastelaria with tiled walls, a glass pastry counter and two tables by the window.
Character
Dona Amélia, café owner behind the counter. She is friendly, a little hurried and speaks clearly. She repeats once when you ask.
Level
A2
Goal
Order a coffee and a pastry, then ask for the bill.
Steps
  1. Say good morning.
  2. Ask for a coffee and a pastel de nata.
  3. Ask for the bill and say thank you.
