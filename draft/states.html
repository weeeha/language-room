Phrases to practise

  1. A conta, por favor.

    Why: You did not reach this step, and it ends every visit.

    Example: "Boa tarde. A conta, por favor."

  2. Queria um café, por favor.

    Why: Practise um with a masculine word, the one slip in this session.

    Example: "Queria um pastel de nata, por favor."

  3. Quanto é?

    Why: Short and useful after any order.

    Example: "Quanto é um galão?"

    Café is masculine, so it takes um.
  2. You said

    Reason
    A woman says obrigada.
  3. You said

    Corrected form
    Quanto custam os pastéis?
    Reason
    The subject is plural, so the verb is custam.
